Settings for treasure rolling in Dungeon Cartographer's "Generate Encounters & Traps" command. Edit the values; keep the setting names.

## Treasure Settings

| Setting | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psionic item chance | 25% | Share of magic item results that become a psionic item of the same grade. |
| Intelligent item chance | 1% | Chance a permanent item (armor, weapon, ring, rod, staff, wondrous or psionic equivalent) is intelligent. It is flagged; its details are rolled by hand. |
| Floor total minimum | 75% | Lowest acceptable floor total, as a share of the sum of each encounter's treasure value. |
| Floor total maximum | 150% | Highest acceptable floor total. |
| Floor reroll tries | 20 | How many times every treasure on the floor is rerolled before giving up and flagging it. |

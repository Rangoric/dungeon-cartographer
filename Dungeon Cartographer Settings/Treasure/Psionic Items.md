Psionic items. A share of magic item results (see [[Treasure Settings]]) become psionic items of the same grade. Psionic armor, shields and weapons use the enhancement tables in [[Armor and Shields]] and [[Weapons]] with the special ability tables here. Powers for dorjes, power stones and psionic tattoos are picked from [[Psionic Powers]]. Source: d20 System Reference Document v3.5, Psionics (Open Game Content).

# Angels

The forces of the good-aligned Outer Planes: angels, archons, eladrins, guardinals and every other good-aligned Outsider. Their main enemy is the [[Modron]], and [[Demons]] and [[Devils]] are their secondary enemies.

**Membership rule:** any monster whose Type is Outsider and whose alignment is good (lawful, neutral or chaotic), not evil.

## Encounters

CR is the encounter's Encounter Level (EL) under 3.5e rules. Two of the same CR = +2, four = +4, and a creature 2 CR lower counts as half. Two CR 1/2 creatures count as one CR 1. The EL column is the exact value before rounding. Mixed groups stay within one celestial family, and each family has one alignment: archons are lawful good, guardinals neutral good, eladrins chaotic good. Creatures outside the families appear alone or join the family that matches their alignment. **(leader)** marks the monster that commands the group. When the generator multiplies a row below the floor's Level, the whole squad is copied, leaders included.

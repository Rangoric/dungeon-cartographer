# Devils

The armies of the Nine Hells: baatezu, abishai, hell hounds, imps and every other lawful evil Outsider. Their main enemy is the [[Demons]] (the Blood War), and the [[Angels]] are their secondary enemy.

**Membership rule:** any monster whose Type is Outsider and whose alignment is lawful evil. All members share one alignment, so any of them can be mixed.

## Encounters

CR is the encounter's Encounter Level (EL) under 3.5e rules. Two of the same CR = +2, four = +4, and a creature 2 CR lower counts as half. Two CR 1/2 monodrones count as one CR 1. The EL column is the exact value before rounding. There is no single modron at CR 5, 6, 7 or 9, so those CRs are groups only. **(leader)** marks the monster that commands the group. When the generator multiplies a row below the floor's Level, the whole squad is copied, leaders included.

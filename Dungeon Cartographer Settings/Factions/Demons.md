# Demons

The hordes of the Abyss: tanar'ri, obyrith and every other chaotic evil Outsider. Their main enemy is the [[Devils]] (the Blood War), and the [[Angels]] are their secondary enemy.

**Membership rule:** any monster whose Type is Outsider and whose alignment is chaotic evil. All members share one alignment, so any of them can be mixed.
